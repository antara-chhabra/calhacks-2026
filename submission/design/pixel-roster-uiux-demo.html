Fight Card Pixel Roster

The six house fighters redrawn as pixel art. Every frame below is rendered live from the arena's own skeleton, poses and colours, so this is what the fight will look like. Nothing about matches, scoring or wiring changes.

What the guide changed

  • Faces are the brightest area, with a dark band under the chin.
  • Four-shade ramps per colour: shadows lean violet to match the arena sky, highlights lean warm.
  • Clean clusters: few colours per part, folds on the tunic and skirt.
  • Weight-shifted stance, angled boots, weapon held forward.

How they plug in

Each body part is drawn separately (torso, arms, legs, head, face, helmet, weapon, emblem) at the same pivots the current art uses, so every existing animation drives them unchanged. Limbs rotate on the same pixel grid, so edges stay crisp in motion.

Overlap fix (with this change)

Today the scoreboard covers the heads, names and speech bubbles of the two fighters on the floating platforms. The scoreboard moves into the clear sky band between them, so nothing overlaps the fighters.
